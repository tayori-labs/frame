# Prompt vocabulary

Prompt order: camera -> lens -> director / style -> shot + move -> subject + action -> lighting -> palette + mood.

## Lighting
| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| high key lighting | Even, low contrast — happy scenes |
| low key lighting | Uneven, high contrast — dark, dramatic |
| chiaroscuro | Extreme low-key contrast — mystery, danger |
| hard light | Directional, sharp shadows — intense |
| soft light | Diffused, few shadows — romantic, gentle |
| volumetric lighting | God rays through dust, fog, rain — memorable shots |
| bokeh | Out-of-focus light — isolation, dreamy |
| silhouette | Subject against bright background — mystery, hidden identity |

## Shot size
| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| extreme wide shot | Sense of place and time — openers |
| wide shot | Subject in relation to environment |
| full shot | Introduce character, body language |
| medium shot | Dialogue, conversational distance |
| close-up | Emotion, detail |
| extreme close-up | Tension, intimacy, tiny details |

## Camera angle
| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| eye-level shot | Direct connection, neutrality |
| high angle shot | Subject feels small, vulnerable |
| low angle shot | Powerful, dominant, heroic |
| bird's-eye view | Overview, scale, relationships |
| worm's-eye view | Larger than life, viewer feels vulnerable |
| dutch angle | Unease, disorientation, chaos |
| over-the-shoulder shot | POV between two characters, confrontation |
| point of view (POV) shot | What the character sees — immersion |

## Camera movement
| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| slow push in | Builds tension / focus |
| dolly back with slight arc | Reveal from a detail |
| whip zoom / whip pan | Fast energy, transitions |
| tracking shot | Follow the subject at speed |
| lateral trucking shot | Side-on, background streaks past |
| jib up / crane | Majestic reveal of scale |
| orbit | Hero moment, 3D presence |
| handheld | Raw, urgent, real (UGC, action) |
| rack focus | Shift attention between subjects |
| locked-off | Still, clinical, controlled |

## Annotated examples
### Epic sci-fi
- CAMERA: Shot on ARRI Alexa 65,
- LENS: Panavision Sphero 65 40mm lens, anamorphic lens flare,
- DIRECTOR / STYLE: in the style of Denis Villeneuve, Blade Runner 2049 vibe.
- SHOT + MOVE: Extreme wide shot, slow majestic jib up.
- SUBJECT + ACTION: A lone figure in a long charcoal coat walks through a vast orange dust storm, dwarfed by a colossal weathered statue half-buried in sand.
- LIGHTING: Diffused sunless glow, silhouette against glowing amber haze, dust suspended in the air.
- PALETTE + MOOD: Saturated burnt-orange and ochre palette, deep shadow, silent and monumental.

Full prompt:
```
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, Panavision Sphero 65 40mm lens, anamorphic lens flare, in the style of Denis Villeneuve, Blade Runner 2049 vibe. Extreme wide shot, slow majestic jib up. A lone figure in a long charcoal coat walks through a vast orange dust storm, dwarfed by a colossal weathered statue half-buried in sand. Diffused sunless glow, silhouette against glowing amber haze, dust suspended in the air. Saturated burnt-orange and ochre palette, deep shadow, silent and monumental.
```

### Noir thriller
- CAMERA: Shot on RED V-Raptor,
- LENS: Zeiss Master Prime 32mm lens, deep focus,
- DIRECTOR / STYLE: in the style of David Fincher, Zodiac vibe.
- SHOT + MOVE: Locked-off symmetrical medium shot, eye-level.
- SUBJECT + ACTION: A man in a rumpled shirt sits alone in a 1970s newsroom at 2am, surrounded by paper stacks, a pen pressed against his lip mid-thought.
- LIGHTING: Low key lighting, cold green-tinted fluorescent overheads, hard shadows under the eyes.
- PALETTE + MOOD: Sickly institutional greens and greys, clinical stillness, dread underneath.

Full prompt:

```
Shot on RED V-Raptor, Zeiss Master Prime 32mm lens, deep focus, in the style of David Fincher, Zodiac vibe. Locked-off symmetrical medium shot, eye-level. Low key lighting, cold green-tinted fluorescent overheads, hard shadows under the eyes. Sickly institutional greens and greys, clinical stillness, dread underneath.
```

### Whimsical comedy

- LIGHTING: High key lighting, soft even light, no harsh shadows.
- PALETTE + MOOD: Pastel pink, mustard and powder blue, whimsical and deadpan.

### Candid UGC
- CAMERA: Shot on iPhone 15 Pro,
- LENS: front camera, 24mm wide-angle lens,
- DIRECTOR / STYLE: candid social-media creator style.
- SHOT + MOVE: Handheld selfie close-up at arm's length, subtle natural shake.
- SUBJECT + ACTION: A 26-year-old woman with curly dark hair laughs as she holds a jar of face cream up to the camera in her small bathroom.
- LIGHTING: Soft light from a frosted window, natural skin texture.
- PALETTE + MOOD: Warm natural tones, casual, slightly imperfect framing, 9:16 vertical.

Full prompt:
```
Shot on iPhone 15 Pro, front camera, 24mm wide-angle lens, candid social-media creator style. Handheld selfie close-up at arm's length, subtle natural shake. A 26-year-old woman with curly dark hair laughs as she holds a jar of face cream up to the camera in her small bathroom. Soft light from a frosted window, natural skin texture. Warm natural tones, casual, slightly imperfect framing, 9:16 vertical.
```
